About

Engineer by training, PM by choice.

I grew up in Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar, studied Aerospace Engineering at IIT Kharagpur, and found out on the Kshitij tech fest team that I enjoyed working where technology, people and business meet more than anything else.

That led me to an MBA at IIM Lucknow, internships in growth, strategy and sales, and now to product. At Kaabil Finance I work on the systems that move a loan from a field officer's first visit to disbursal and collections.
