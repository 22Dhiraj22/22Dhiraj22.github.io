Making the sanction-to-disbursal funnel visible

Approved loans were getting stuck between sanction and disbursal, and nobody could say where or for how long. I gave leadership one self-serve view of every stage, by branch.

RoleProduct Manager
Timeline3 weeks · 2026

01 · ContextA loan has many owners

In a lending NBFC, a loan passes through Sales, Credit, Operations and Finance on its way from sanction to disbursal. Each team saw only its own step. Leadership received weekly Excel MIS sheets that were stitched together by hand, out of date when they arrived, and often didn't agree with each other.

02 · Problem"Where are the loans stuck?" had no answer

I spoke to regional heads, the credit team and operations. The same three pain points came up every time:

  • No single source of truth. Every team counted "sanctioned" differently.
  • Slow reporting. MIS took about 3 hours a week to compile and was always a week old.
  • No branch view. You couldn't tell a slow branch from a slow process.

"By the time I see the report, the customer has already called the branch three times."

03 · DiscoveryStarting from the data, not the dashboard

Before designing any charts, I mapped how each stage is stored in the database and found two problems that would have quietly broken any dashboard:

  • Duplicate branch records (legacy "_A" copies) that double-counted volume.
  • A head-office code that represented the whole company and had to be treated separately from branches.

I wrote down metric definitions (what counts as "sanctioned" and "disbursed", which date applies) and got Credit and Ops to agree on them before any build started.

04 · OptionsWhat we considered

A. Automate the Excel MISQuick, but it keeps the "report arrives weekly" model and doesn't allow drill-down.
B. Build reports inside the core platformBest long term, but it would take months of engineering time from a busy roadmap.
C. Self-serve BI on Metabase (chosen)Open source, connects directly to our data, and ready in weeks. If the questions change, it's cheap to throw away.

05 · SolutionOne funnel, every branch

  • A stage-by-stage funnel from sanction to disbursal with conversion between each stage.
  • Branch and region filters to separate process problems from branch problems.
  • The dashboard is built from code (a Python script), so it can be recreated and changed without manual clicking.
  • No customer data (PII) on the dashboard: only aggregate counts and amounts are shown, never borrower details.

06 · ImpactWhat changed

  • Weekly manual MIS work went from ~3 hours to zero.
  • Leadership reviews now start from a shared screen instead of comparing spreadsheets.
  • The agreed metric definitions became the reference for other reports.

07 · LearningsWhat I'd do differently

  • Agreeing on definitions is most of the work. The charts took days; agreeing on what "sanctioned" means took two weeks.
  • Start with one decision. Next time I'd design around the single question leaders ask each week, then expand.
