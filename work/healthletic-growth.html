Doubling subscriptions by fixing a leaky signup funnel

People were visiting the site but not subscribing, especially on mobile. I traced where users dropped off between search and signup, fixed the friction points, and measured the impact with data.

RoleDigital Marketing / Growth Intern
CompanyHealthletic (health & fitness)
AreasSEO, CRO, Mobile UX
ToolsGA, PageSpeed, WordPress, Figma

01 · ProblemTraffic without conversion

The site was getting visitors, but many, especially on mobile, left before subscribing. Pages were slow, navigation was cluttered, and the path to signup wasn't clear.

02 · ApproachFix the leak at each stage

  1. Visibility (Search → Click): rewrote meta titles and descriptions, improved internal linking and local SEO, and moved blog topics toward high-intent keywords like "plan" and "program".
  2. Conversion (Visit → Subscribe): A/B tested CTA wording, hero messaging and creative formats; ran multi-channel campaigns across blogs, email and social.
  3. Mobile UX and speed: used PageSpeed Insights to find slow pages, cut heavy assets, simplified layouts through a WordPress–Figma workflow, and shortened the signup path.

03 · MeasurementProving it worked

I compared before and after averages in Google Analytics: new subscriptions, sessions per user, time on site and organic CTR. Conversions grew without extra spend, which also made customer acquisition cheaper.

04 · ImpactResults

  • Subscriptions ~2× baseline over the campaign window.
  • Organic traffic and CTR +15%.
  • Engagement +40%, average session duration +25%.
  • Page load time ~30% faster.

05 · LearningsWhat it taught me about product

  • Think of the journey as a funnel. Most growth problems are drop-off problems.
  • Speed is a feature. On mobile, every extra second costs signups.
